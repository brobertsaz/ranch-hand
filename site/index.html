From one hand to the next

The radio call is gone by supper. The pin isn't.

  1. Step 1 · Out in the pasture 10sec

    Pin what you find

    Snap a photo with the ear tag in frame and tap what's wrong: sick, feed, water, fence or gate. GPS and time fill in on their own, and the buttons work with gloves on.

  2. Step 2 · Still no bars 0bars

    Saved on the phone right away

    Nothing waits on a signal. The photo, the note and the pin are on your phone the moment you tap save, marked as waiting to sync.

Who's building this

Built after a week in the Nebraska Sandhills

I'm Bob Roberts. In the summer of 2026 I spent a week on a working cattle ranch in the Nebraska Sandhills, riding every day, checking cattle and riding along for haying. One problem stuck with me: a hand spots a sick calf or a stuck float where there's no signal, and the next person out can't find it again.

I'm not a rancher. I've built software for more than 15 years, and I also run Ranch Brand Book, a free directory of working ranches you can visit. Ranch Hand is the tool I wanted that week: offline-first from day one, buttons big enough for gloves, and built on open maps so a ranch's pins aren't locked into anybody's platform.

In June 2027 it rides out with working hands on a Wyoming ranch: real cattle, real feed runs and no cell signal. What they say decides what gets built next, and ranches on the list get the next spots to try it.

If something on your place gets lost between the pasture and the house, I'd like to hear about it. Email comes straight to me.
