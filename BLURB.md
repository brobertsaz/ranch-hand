**Ranch Hand** is a phone app for working ranches where there's no cell signal. A hand on horseback snaps a photo of a sick cow, a low water tank or a broken fence, taps what's wrong with gloves on, and drops a pin. It's saved on the phone right away and syncs to the whole crew when they're back in range. Feed stations, tanks and gates become permanent places with a running history. **Take me there** points an arrow straight to any pin, with no roads or signal needed, so whoever rides out next can find it.
